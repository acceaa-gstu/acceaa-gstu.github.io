About the Association

The Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering Alumni Association, GSTU (ACCEAA, GSTU) is a non-political, non-profit, and voluntary organization composed of individuals who have completed graduation from the Department of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering (ACCE), Gopalganj Science and Technology University (GSTU). It was formally adopted by its founding members at a General Assembly, with the shared purpose of building a lasting bond of friendship and cooperation among graduates, accelerating the professional development of its members, and contributing meaningfully to the advancement of the Department and the University.

Our Goal

"To build a permanent, self-sustaining bridge between the Department and every graduate who has passed through it — one that outlives any single batch or committee, and makes ACCEAA, GSTU the natural first place an alumnus turns to for mentorship, connection, and belonging."
04

Our Function

In practice, the Association carries out its objectives through a defined governing structure and a regular calendar of activity:

Holding an Annual General Meeting to review activities and approve accounts.
Organizing at least one annual reunion, along with seminars, workshops, and social events.
Running career counselling, mentorship, scholarships, and internship support for current students.
Maintaining a public alumni directory and record of members' contributions and achievements.
Managing Association funds transparently, with quarterly accounts and an annual audit.
Electing a new Executive Committee every two years through an independent Election Commission.
05

Our Achievement

As a newly founded association, our early achievements have been foundational: successfully forming a founding Executive Committee with representation from every graduated batch, adopting our founding Constitution, and beginning the work of building a unified alumni directory. We are now preparing for our first Annual General Meeting and our first full committee election, alongside our first organized reunion — the first of many milestones we look forward to sharing here.
